Privacy Policy

The Digital Shelf · Last updated November 18, 2026

The Digital Shelf (“the app”) is a personal book organizer for Android, developed by Capseran (“we”). The app has no accounts. It is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Your library stays on your device

Your books, shelves, tags, notes, quotes, reading history, loans, goals, wishlist, prices and profile (name and bio) are stored only on your device. We do not run a server and we never receive this data.

Book search and covers

When you search for a book, scan an ISBN or browse Explore, the app sends your search words or the ISBN to Open Library (run by the Internet Archive) and to the Google Books API to find titles, covers, ratings and e-book prices. Covers are loaded from these services. These requests never include your library or profile. They are handled under the Internet Archive’s terms and privacy policy and Google’s privacy policy.

  • E-book price checks include your country code so Google Books can show local prices.
  • If you add your own Google Books API key in Settings, it is sent to Google with these requests.
  • Search words are sent exactly as you type them. If you type an email address or other personal details into a search, that text is sent too, so search only by title, author or ISBN.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only while you scan a barcode or capture text from a page. Barcode scanning and text recognition run on your device with Google ML Kit. The images are not uploaded. Photos you choose as book covers are stored only on your device. The app only accesses photos and files that you pick yourself.

Google ML Kit diagnostics

The on-device ML Kit libraries may send Google limited technical information, such as device and app details, performance metrics and a per-installation identifier, to keep the features working and to measure their use. This never includes your images, scanned text or library. See Google’s ML Kit data disclosure.

Ads

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects your device’s IP address (which may be used to estimate your general location), your advertising ID and app set ID, your interactions with ads and the app (such as app launches, taps and video views) and app performance information, and shares them with Google. It never receives your library, notes or profile. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google’s Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

  • If you live in a region that requires it, such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before ads use your data. You can change your choices at any time in the app under Settings > Privacy choices.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device’s settings (Google > Ads, or Security & privacy > Privacy > Ads on some devices).
  • Watching an optional video in Settings hides all ads for 24 hours. The end time of this pass is stored only on your device.
  • While Digital Shelf Premium is active, the app shows no ads and does not start the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

Premium purchases

Digital Shelf Premium is sold through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment under the Google Play Terms of Service and Google’s privacy policy, and we never receive your payment details. The app asks Google Play whether your Google account has an active Premium purchase and stores the answer on your device, so ads stay off when you are offline. Nothing about your purchase is sent to us. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions.

Sharing, export and import

Nothing leaves the app unless you choose to share it. When you export a file, create a backup or share your profile card, it goes only to the app or location you pick. Importing reads only the file you select. Store links, such as Amazon or Bookshop.org, open in your browser, and those sites have their own privacy policies.

Backups

If Android backup is turned on, Android may include the app’s data in your personal device backup in your Google account. We cannot access that backup. You can also create a backup file yourself in the app under Settings > Import, export & backup.

Security

All network requests made by the app use encrypted HTTPS connections.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything at any time with Settings > Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app. Because we never receive your library, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. Data collected for ads is handled by Google under its privacy policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. The current version is also available inside the app under Settings > Privacy policy.
